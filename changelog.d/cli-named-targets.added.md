- The operator configuration can name targets: each one a complete binding, a direct graph or cluster root at a local path, or a server with its credential owner. Cloud storage keeps its `--store` and `--cluster` addresses, since a target cannot yet pin the endpoint and account the environment supplies. `--target NAME`, else `defaults.target`, addresses a command, `target list|show|add|remove` lists and edits them one entry at a time while keeping the rest of the file, `login --target` stores a target's token, and `credential get --target` hands it to another program through a pipe. `version --json` reports their output versions under `protocols`. Existing `servers:`, `profiles:` and their flags keep working. See [named targets][cli-named-targets].
