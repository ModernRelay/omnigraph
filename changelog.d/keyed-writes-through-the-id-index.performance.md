- A keyed `insert`, an `update ... where <key> = v`, a Blob cell write and a `load --mode merge` of rows that are all new or all present now reach their rows through the `id` index instead of reading the `id` column of every fragment. On an indexed table of 262,144 rows, a one-row write read 4.8 MB in up to 212 storage requests and now reads under 0.1 MB in at most 34; the cost no longer grows with the table. Results are unchanged.
