- `omnigraph access` says who you are at a target and which actions its policy grants, graph-wide, on `--branch` and from it to `--target-branch`. A server answers through its access route; for direct storage the CLI decides with a cluster graph's applied policy for the declared actor, and a standalone graph allows every action; a cluster graph that is not applied or is quarantined refuses. See [what you may do at a target][cli-access-command].
