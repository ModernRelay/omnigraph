- An `rrf(...)` order now follows the one total order every search order has (RFC 0047): rows sort by the fused score, then the keys written after `rrf(...)`, then every binding's id, and `limit` counts rows after that order instead of fused entities. `rrf(...)` is projectable in `return` under the same rule as `nearest` and `bm25` (`T33`), as an `F64` named `<var>._rrf` without an alias; the `T37` refusal is withdrawn. A search-led aggregate orders its groups by the keys after the search function, then every group key. `asc` on a `bm25(...)` or `rrf(...)` order key is refused (`T54`). `.gqt` cases may `expect ordered` an `rrf()`-led order and a search-led aggregate order with a key after the search function.
